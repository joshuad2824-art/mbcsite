Who we are

“Come to me, all you who are weary and burdened, and I will give you rest. Take my yoke upon you and learn from me, for I am gentle and humble in heart, and you will find rest for your souls.”

Matthew 11:28–30

Our vision

Our vision is to see the church live in faithful obedience for the glory of God and the good of all people. We believe every member matters, and that God has given each person a gifting and a role.

We pray God would use us to make disciples from our neighbors to the nations, and teach us to love Him and one another more and more faithfully.
